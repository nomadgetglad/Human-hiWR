Human answers, runs commands in the app, shows graphs, routes mail and calendar events to the right side, and flips action toggles you're allowed to change. Sample menu with sample data from the demos. HiWR is the record: what people put in. Human works from it and puts drafts out to a person, who approves. Every send for approval and every sign-off coming back also lands in the approvals chat under Scheduling. Human never moves money, places an order or changes access on its own.
