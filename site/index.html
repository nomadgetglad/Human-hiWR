A day with Human

Same core records, different day

    You stay in control

    It proposes. It never moves money on its own.

    Human can't pay, refund, change payroll or text a customer by itself. These rules hold for every business on it.

    Two people approve money

    Refunds, credits, chargebacks, fees and payroll changes wait for two certified approvers. The person who asked can't be one of them.

    You set access

    Only the account holder sets levels. Staff without accounting access see a ticket's status, not the money on it.

    Certifications pass down

    A certified employee can certify someone for one task. Nobody hands out more access than they hold.

    Every result gets checked

    After an action, the person responsible is asked whether it worked and did what they meant.

    Both numbers, side by side

    When a ticket and the books disagree, a manager sees both values and their sources. The app doesn't pick a winner.

    Less personal data

    No full card numbers. No passwords: sign-in uses an authenticator app. History is never overwritten.

    Where it runs

    You choose where your data lives

    On site

    The AI runs on a Windows desktop at your business. Data stays in the building.

    Most businesses

    Hybrid

    Routine work runs on site. Heavier planning goes to the cloud, with names swapped for labels before anything leaves.

    Cloud

    Hosted for you with nothing to install. Good for several locations or owners who want no upkeep.
